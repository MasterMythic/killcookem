A White House buddy comedy. A savage alien war. A neon death casino.

When animal-like aliens destroy Earth’s food supply, Kill Cook’em discovers their flesh can heal humans and grant extraordinary powers. Five years later, he attempts to end the war by building 21Zero—a casino where humans gamble blood and aliens gamble their bodies.

CAMERA

Heroic scale. Comic precision.

Muscular action photographed with clarity, punctured by character comedy rather than undercut by it.
